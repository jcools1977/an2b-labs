## The situation

TR-002 as written asks whether representational geometry converges
across open model families (CKA/Procrustes) and whether convergence
predicts coupling cost. FRONTIER-001 reports the existence question
answered at macro scale: vec2vec (arXiv:2505.12540) constructively
demonstrates unsupervised cross-architecture embedding translation
with no paired data, mini-vec2vec (arXiv:2510.02348) makes the
alignment cheap, and the paradigm has jumped substrate to fMRI
(arXiv:2605.20496). Running TR-002 as written risks a
micro-of-a-macro: confirming at desk scale what the field has
already shown at full scale.

## The options

### (a) Rescope: desk-scale replication of vec2vec-class claims
On the lab's pinned 4-bit small models, with our corpus sizes.
The genuinely untested niche: nobody has published whether the
universal-geometry claims SURVIVE quantization and small n. A
verification result either way is in the lab's lane (verification,
refutation, instruments). Shape: new frozen protocol revision, new
thresholds pre-registered around replication fidelity, oracle
re-sealed against the surviving text.
- Cost profile: still the heaviest downloads in the program (5-6
  model families), but 4-bit desk-runnable throughout.
- Relation to the slate: preserves Track A's coupling thread;
  TR-001b's per-token gap remains open and separate (FRONTIER-001:
  vec2vec is embedding-level, not hidden-state transfer).

### (b) Rescope: the failure-boundary audit
Not "does universal geometry exist" but WHERE it breaks at desk
scale: across quantization levels, model sizes, layer depths, and
domain shifts, mapped with pre-registered break criteria. The
deliverable is a boundary map, the kind of instrument result the
majors' existence papers do not supply.
- Cost profile: similar downloads, more conditions per model, the
  longest analysis of the three options.
- Relation to the slate: feeds the same downstream interests
  (coupling, EngramPort's translation-fidelity question flagged for
  TR-003) with sharper edges.

### (c) Retire TR-002; advance TR-006
Record TR-002 as retired-by-radar (the first slate change driven by
the macro-check, itself a precedent worth the ledger's respect), and
advance TR-006 in the execution order.
- Cost profile: zero spent on a crowded question.
- Relation to the slate: Wave 2 loses its Track A entry; the
  vec2vec-derived questions migrate to TR-003's already-flagged
  rescope (what survives translation, not whether it exists).

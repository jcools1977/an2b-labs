**Ruling: credibility-asset. RATIFIED by the PI (J. DeVere Cooley), 2026-08-31.**

The finding's value is the publication itself, and it is the first
FAIL to earn this category on merit rather than by default. TR-011
manufactured the exact number the field loves (a perfect AUC for
"detecting publishable prose") and then confiscated it with a control
pre-registered before the number existed. "The lab that catches its
own counterfeits" is worth more to Covenant Systems, the SBIR and
government story, and the lab's standing than a weak PASS would have
been, because it demonstrates the property buyers of verification
actually pay for: machinery that binds its own hands before temptation
exists.

Secondary utilization, named without being promoted: the entropy-trace
visualizer ships into the writing toolchain as a texture instrument,
honestly labeled (it reads style and subject, not craft), and the
author-identity control enters the lab's standing methods kit
alongside TR-020's bite-proofs. What was learned that is worth
building: controls before classifiers, always; identity is the
confound that eats prose metrics; and a FAIL with receipts compounds,
while an unexamined AUC decays.

# TR-003r utilization verdict (draft for PI ratification; CREDO section 3)

**Ruling proposed: credibility-asset.**

What was learned that is worth building: a memory store does not
survive zero-fit translation between embedding models at desk scale,
even between near-twin 384-dimensional encoders, and the loss is in
the anchor coordinates themselves (measurable inside a single model,
proportional to anisotropy) rather than in the crossing. The
paired-anchor ceiling on the same anchors keeps 69 to 89 percent of
recall and most provenance, so portability is a FITTED operation with
a measured anchor-count curve, never a coordinate trick; and a fit on
too few anchors fails the dangerous way, returning wrong memories
with native-grade confidence 9 to 30 percent of the time, while the
zero-fit method fails the safe way and almost always. Two
instruments join the methods kit: the raw-vector baseline beside any
cross-encoder translation claim (BERT-family encoders share
coordinates enough to retrieve at 0.17 to 0.54 with no transform),
and the scrambled-anchor arm as the catcher for geometry
masquerading as alignment (it outperformed real anchors). The
finding's value is the publication: the first measured loss profile
of a translated memory store at desk scale, with the ceiling, the
floor that was not a floor, and the safety asymmetry all in one
table. What it feeds downstream is for the harvest review at the
wave boundary, on the PI's word.

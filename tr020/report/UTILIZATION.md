# TR-020 utilization verdict (CREDO)

**Ruling: product-now.** DRAFTED 2026-08-28, awaiting PI ratification.

The audit harness ships as `deadwood-audit` (CLI `deadwood`), and the
experiment's own results built its pitch, every clause with a receipt:
your defaults are probably fine (0/8 dead across the four most-popular
frameworks' canonical configurations); your accretions are what need
auditing (the folklore about bloat describes what teams bolt on after
the tutorial, exactly the systems this tool points at); and the cheap
shortcut you would prefer does not work (textual-influence tracing
refuted at kappa 0.29 with pre-frozen thresholds, mechanism
demonstrated), which is precisely why the real audit earns its run
time. Secondary asset: the sealed seven-system benchmark is released
as a standing exam for anyone claiming a cheaper tracer, which makes
the negative result infrastructure rather than a footnote.

What was learned that is worth building: intervention is the only
trustworthy dead-component detector; bite-proofs make foreign-framework
auditing trustable; capability-matched placebos and split placebo arms
are required instrument design for any successor. Packaging exists in
the repo; PyPI publication is a separate PI-authorized act.

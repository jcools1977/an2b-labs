# TR-015 utilization draft (PI ratifies; nothing here is decided)

**Proposed verdict: credibility-asset.**

The PASS-gated deliverable (a voice-drift meter for the editing
pipeline) died with the verdict: there is no subspace to meter at
desk scale, and shipping one anyway would be selling the costume
TR-011 and TR-015 just took off two different instruments.

What the FAIL yields:

1. **The methods kit gains its third control.** TR-001 gave the
   shuffled-pairing control, TR-011 the author-identity control,
   TR-015 the topic-factor control: before claiming a latent voice
   representation, show that a classifier on the topic factors alone
   does not out-attribute your embedding. Here it did, 0.64 vs 0.42,
   twenty dimensions beating three hundred eighty-four. One line to
   run, fatal when it fires, unreported in the literature the same
   way TR-011's control was.
2. **The credibility story compounds.** Four pre-registered verdicts
   in eight days, three FAILs and a SPLIT, every seductive number
   confiscated by machinery built before the number existed. The
   Wave 1 narrative extends without edits.
3. **Burrows-still-wins is a citable desk-scale result** for anyone
   building attribution or style tooling on small embedders, and the
   sealed corpus builder (14 authors, works held out whole, frozen
   split) is a reusable exam for embedding-based stylometry claims.

Feeds, does not gate: the Epoch trajectory machinery (coordinates on
a shared plane) remains available to the writing toolchain as an
honestly-labeled instrument, same covenant terms, if the PI ever
wants it; the FAIL says its plane holds topic residue, so any such
use must carry that label.

# TR-002r utilization draft (PI ratifies; nothing here is decided)

**Proposed verdict: credibility-asset.**

The radar sent this experiment to the one niche the frontier left
open — desk-scale verification of the universal-geometry claims —
and the niche paid: the first published boundary for the vec2vec
literature, with the artifact machinery to make it stick.

What the FAIL yields:

1. **A citable negative with unusual precision**: unsupervised
   embedding translation, as published, does not survive 4-bit
   small-model spaces on single-register corpora at n <= 16k, even
   between near-twin encoders, while supervised alignment works
   everywhere. The failure is located (stage one, anchoring), the
   preconditions were pre-registered before the data spoke, and the
   n-above-16k question is cleanly posed for anyone with the
   compute.
2. **The centered-companion rule as standing equipment**: raw cosine
   0.86 with zero retrieval, manufactured and unmasked in one table.
   Every embedding-translation claim now has a two-line honesty
   check this lab can cite as its own receipt.
3. **The class-stratified convergence census is a small positive
   finding riding the negative**: decoder-decoder geometry aligns
   best at desk scale, quantization does not matter, corpus and
   architecture class do. Feeds TR-003's already-flagged rescope
   (what survives translation) with measured priors.
4. **The certification-exam pattern hardened**: it refuted a
   published pipeline's solver against a known-true optimum before
   any real data was spent. The seeded-growth matcher is a reusable
   artifact in its own right.
5. **The meta-lab compounding**: first radar-driven rescope, first
   kickoff-gate adjudication, first pre-authorized mid-flight
   fallback executing under hardware pressure — the governance
   machinery ran a full cycle under load and every seam held or
   was logged where it strained.

# CREDO.md - Solutions First, Harvest Second
## How AN2B Labs findings become AN2B value, and the firewall that keeps the order straight

**Standing document. Binds every lab session alongside CLAUDE.md.**
**Ratified by the PI (J. DeVere Cooley), 2026-08-27.**

## 1. The credence

We build solutions first. Economic value follows good solutions because the
two are correlated, and the causation runs one way: quality can generate
economics, but economics cannot generate quality. The same one-way structure
as TR-020's placebo asymmetry, applied to the lab itself.

Value is a byproduct of true findings, never a design input to experiments.

## 2. The firewall (non-negotiable)

Product pressure never enters an experiment in flight. Concretely:

- Hypothesis selection, thresholds, kill criteria, controls, and
  interpretation are chosen for scientific reasons only. No protocol is
  written, and no frozen criterion is read, with a product outcome in mind.
- No session proposes a mid-experiment change for product reasons. If a
  finding looks commercially exciting mid-run, that excitement is logged
  nowhere and acted on never, until the verdict is in.
- A clean negative plus a credibility brick is a fully successful outcome.
  The pressure to find value must never bend the next experiment's design.
  A near-miss is a FAIL; a valueless finding is still a finding.

The firewall that keeps thresholds frozen before data is the same firewall
that keeps product hopes out of hypothesis selection. BB4C applies to the
harvest too: breath before the pivot.

## 3. The harvest mechanism

Findings decay. The harvest runs per wave, not at program end.

**Per experiment:** every TR closes with a utilization verdict as a standard
deliverable, written when the report ships, while the finding is hot. One
paragraph, exactly one ruling:

- **product-now** - the finding or its tool ships as or into a product
  (e.g. TR-019 provenance receipts; deadwood after the wild phase)
- **feeds-product-X** - the finding changes a named product's design or
  becomes an instrument in an existing toolchain (e.g. TR-003 verdict
  feeds EngramPort's portability story either way; TR-011/TR-016 feed the
  writing toolchain; TR-013 feeds long-session agent infrastructure;
  Track B results feed ClawTex architecture)
- **credibility-asset** - the finding's value is the publication itself:
  proof of method for Covenant Systems, the SBIR/government story, and the
  lab's standing. TR-001's FAIL is the founding example.

The verdict goes in the TR's report directory and is referenced from the
report's closing section. It states what was learned that is worth building,
not how to monetize the paper.

**Per wave:** at each wave boundary, a harvest review reads the accumulated
verdicts and decides what actually gets built next. That decision is the
PI's. Sessions prepare the material; they do not green-light product work.

## 4. Session obligations

1. When closing any TR, draft the utilization verdict as part of the
   standard deliverables, subject to PI ratification like any judgment call.
2. At a wave boundary, flag that a harvest review is due and assemble the
   verdicts. Do not begin product implementation from lab findings without
   the PI's explicit word.
3. Never cite commercial value in any DECISIONS entry, protocol reading,
   or threshold interpretation. If a document needs the firewall stated,
   cite this file.
4. The lab's publications remain public, PASS or FAIL, per the master
   program. Harvest never delays or softens a report.

## 5. Why this document exists

Two days into the lab's life, the pattern was already visible: the FAIL
became the founding credential, the red control leg became the most citable
finding, and the tool exists because a real problem was solved honestly.
Build the true thing well and value accrues. Chase the value directly and
you get neither.

*Trust. Walk. Build.*

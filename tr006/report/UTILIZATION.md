# TR-006 utilization verdict (draft for the PI's ratification at the harvest review; CREDO section 3)

**Ruling proposed: credibility-asset.**

What was learned that is worth building: at desk scale a bounded
broadcast buffer is a scratchpad. Three 7B-class models in roles
perform the same at every capacity that binds, the same as an
unlimited log where it does not, and within one to three points of
the best single model; self-assessed salience is indistinguishable
from random numbers; the first broadcast carries the whole effect
and further exchange adds nothing; roles are dead weight on QA and a
synthesizer effect on puzzles. Two instruments join the kit: the
certified regime test (piecewise against smooth, binomial AIC, proven
on step, logistic and flat worlds) and the slot-competition trace that
exposed the arithmetic every capacity sweep must satisfy (candidates
must exceed slots). The finding's value is the publication: a clean,
replicated, controlled null on the GWT bottleneck claim in exactly the
regime the field is building on (FRONTIER-002), with the design
arithmetic that any such claim must clear stated plainly. What it
feeds downstream (Track B's remaining protocols inherit the harness
and the arithmetic) is the harvest review's to rule on.

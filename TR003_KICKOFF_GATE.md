## The situation

TR-003 as written asks whether anchor-relative representations
(Moschella-class) put cross-model retrieval within 10 F1 of native.
FRONTIER-002 finds the existence side established: zero-shot
stitching via shared anchors is literature, refinement is active
(learned anchors + whitened inner products, arXiv:2605.30596; LDIR,
arXiv:2505.10354), and industry has named the pain ("vector drift,"
embedding models turning over every 60-90 days under production
indexes). Run as written, TR-003 risks the micro-of-a-macro that
TR-002 avoided by rescope. Meanwhile TR-002r handed this lane
measured priors: cross-model alignment WORKS with paired anchors at
desk scale (skylines 0.40-1.0, class-stratified), and fails
unsupervised.

## The options

### (a) Rescope: what SURVIVES translation (the FRONTIER-flagged shape)
Not "does anchor retrieval work" but what a MEMORY system keeps and
loses crossing models: recall precision, ranking stability,
provenance survival, and failure asymmetry, measured on a graph-RAG
shaped store at desk scale, against TR-002r's skylines as the
ceiling. The lab's lane (verification, boundaries, instruments) and
the EngramPort design question in one experiment; ARENA.md's road
runs through it.

### (b) Run as written, positioned as desk-scale verification
The 10-F1 gate on the lab's pinned spaces and corpus machinery,
framed as independent verification of the stitching literature under
quantization and small anchors sets, boundary curves over anchor
count. Defensible, but the niche is narrower than (a) and closer to
the crowded existence question.

### (c) Retire TR-003 by radar; fold its question into the arena build
Record the anchor question as answered-enough by the field plus
TR-002r's skylines; let the EngramPort/arena work absorb portability
as engineering with pre-registered acceptance tests rather than a
TR. Cost: Track A loses its coupling capstone; the lab's memory
substrate rides on others' claims unverified at desk scale.
